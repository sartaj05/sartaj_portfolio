About Me

A dedicated Software Developer with 1 year of experience in building robust backend applications using Python and Django (including Django REST Framework). Recently, I have been working with FastAPI for the past 2 months, expanding my skills in developing modern and efficient APIs. Passionate about creating scalable solutions and contributing to innovative projects.

My journey in software development began during my BTech in Information Technology at Chaudhary Charan Singh University, Meerut, where I started working with frontend technologies including HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. During my college years, I also developed data analytics dashboards, which sparked my interest in data-driven solutions. As I progressed through my studies, I transitioned to backend development, where I discovered my passion for creating scalable REST APIs, leveraging caching systems for performance optimization, and writing test-driven, maintainable code using Pytest and SonarQube.

Currently working as a Jr. Software Engineer at Createch Software Pvt. Ltd. for the Ministry of Defence (ASDC), I am involved in developing enterprise-level solutions using cutting-edge technologies. My diverse experience from frontend development to data analytics and now backend engineering gives me a comprehensive understanding of the full development lifecycle. I am passionate about contributing to innovative solutions in web development and AI/ML domains while continuously growing in dynamic environments.
